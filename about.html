Our story

Rooted in Port Harcourt

Nwabuoturi Foundation began as a simple act: paying the school fees of a few children whose parents could not. That act grew into a foundation awarding over a hundred scholarships every year.

We work with schools, community leaders and families across Rivers State to find the children most at risk of dropping out, and to keep them learning - with fees, supplies, food and mentorship.

Our Mission

Keep every less-privileged child in school and learning.

Our Vision

A Rivers State where no child's education ends because of poverty.

Our Values

Compassion, integrity, education and community.
