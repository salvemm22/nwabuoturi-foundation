About Us

Our Mission

Nwabuoturi Foundation began as a simple act: paying the school fees of a few children whose parents could not. That act grew into a foundation awarding over a hundred scholarships every year across Rivers State.

We work with schools, community leaders and families to find the children most at risk of dropping out - and keep them learning with fees, supplies, food and mentorship.

What We Do

Our Vision

A Rivers State where no child's education ends because of poverty. Compassion, integrity, education and community guide everything we do - and every donation is reported back openly to our donors.
