About Us

Our Mission

Across Port Harcourt, thousands of children with big dreams sit at home because their parents cannot pay fees. Nwabuoturi Foundation stands in that gap - we find these children, pay their way, equip them and walk with them every term.

What We Do

Our Vision

A Rivers State where no child's education ends because of poverty. Through four practical programs - scholarships, back-to-school drives, mentorship and welfare support - we remove the exact barriers that push children out of the classroom.
