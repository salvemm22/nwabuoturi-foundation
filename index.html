Why we exist

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

Across Port Harcourt, thousands of children with big dreams sit at home every September because their parents cannot pay fees. Some hawk on the streets during school hours. Many drop out entirely.

Nwabuoturi Foundation was created to stand in that gap. We find these children, pay their way, equip them and walk with them - and we have been doing it every single year, with the support of donors like you.

  • Scholarships for poor pupils
  • Free books and uniforms
  • Nutrition and welfare support
  • Mentorship for teens
  • Community outreach
  • Open reporting to donors
